# KPI Definitions

## Overview

This document defines the key performance indicators (KPIs) used to analyze the sample sales dataset.

The dataset contains the following fields:

- OrderID
- OrderDate
- Customer
- Product
- Quantity
- UnitPrice

These KPIs provide basic measures for analyzing sales performance, order activity, and customer purchasing behavior.

---

## 1. Total Sales

**Business Definition:**  
Total Sales represents the total revenue generated from all sales transactions.

**Calculation:**  

Total Sales = Quantity × UnitPrice, summed across all orders.

**Required Columns:**
- Quantity
- UnitPrice

**Example:**  
If Quantity = 2 and UnitPrice = 750:

Total Sales = 2 × 750 = 1,500

---

## 2. Total Orders

**Business Definition:**  
Total Orders represents the number of unique orders recorded in the dataset.

**Calculation:**  

Count the distinct values of OrderID.

**Required Column:**
- OrderID

**Example:**  
If the dataset contains OrderIDs 1001, 1002, 1003, 1004, and 1005, Total Orders = 5.

---

## 3. Average Order Value

**Business Definition:**  
Average Order Value measures the average revenue generated per order.

**Calculation:**  

Average Order Value = Total Sales / Total Orders

**Required Columns:**
- OrderID
- Quantity
- UnitPrice

---

## 4. Total Quantity Sold

**Business Definition:**  
Total Quantity Sold represents the total number of product units sold across all orders.

**Calculation:**  

Sum of Quantity.

**Required Column:**
- Quantity

**Example:**  
For quantities 2, 3, 5, 10, and 1:

Total Quantity Sold = 21

---

## 5. Sales by Product

**Business Definition:**  
Sales by Product measures the total revenue generated by each product.

**Calculation:**  

For each Product:

Sales Amount = Quantity × UnitPrice

Then sum Sales Amount by Product.

**Required Columns:**
- Product
- Quantity
- UnitPrice

---

## 6. Sales by Customer

**Business Definition:**  
Sales by Customer measures the total revenue generated from each customer.

**Calculation:**  

Sum the Sales Amount for each Customer.

**Required Columns:**
- Customer
- Quantity
- UnitPrice

---

## Data Validation

Before calculating the KPIs, the source data should be checked for:

- Missing or blank values
- Incorrect data types
- Duplicate OrderID values
- Extra whitespace in Customer and Product fields
- Invalid Quantity or UnitPrice values

The Power Query cleaning process should ensure that Quantity and UnitPrice are numeric and OrderDate is stored as a date.

A calculated SalesAmount column will be created using:

SalesAmount = Quantity × UnitPrice

The cleaned query output will be compared with the original source rows to confirm that the transformations and calculated values are correct.
